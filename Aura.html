Aura

Aura is a data platform that ingests machine generated data and generates a contextually relevant shared experience to visualize that data. Aura is currently used by pilots to visualize flight plans and flight data in 3D. The application allows two or more users to see the same experience and includes 3D artifacts such as to-scale topographic maps and annotation tools. Aura is also used to consolidate NASA satellite data streams into a single, shareable experience for space weather monitoring and forecasting. Space weather events such as coronal mass ejections (CMEs) are visualized over a 3D model of our inner solar system.





Customer: USAF Air Mobility Command

While serving as the CTO for Dauntless XR, I oversaw the execution of a SBIR Phase 2 mixed-reality project for the US Air Force Air Mobility Command. The goal of the project was to streamline flight crew debriefs and provide a means to determine any errors or issues that had occurred during the flight. By using instrumentation data from the onboard Flight Data Recorder of the KC-46A refueler, I oversaw the development as we ingested, parsed, and displayed the data to multiple users and empowered the exploration of real time in a collaborative environment. By using a proprietary solution dubbed "Hub-and-Spoke", I was able to design and implement a solution architecture that would offload the processing of data to a dedicated conventional computer while serving out the session data to all connected XR devices to reconstruct and render the display locally. Furthermore, users were able to update the session state by altering the topography, scale, flight display, and choose a point in time with an intuitive timeline control system turning a tedious process into an immersive data playground by utilizing the inherent 4D nature of the data. Users were also able to display an accurate representation of the primary flight display, allowing for instructors to verify flight errors with pinpoint accuracy by rendering what the air crew saw in the cockpit itself. Users were also able to make whiteboard-style annotations in mixed reality to allow for further collaboration while in the session.

Customer: NASA

Aura: Space Weather

Space weather visualizer using publicly available data sources

I also oversaw the execution of a NASA SBIR Phase 1 where we took the same technology that powered the USAF solution and visualized space weather data. The goal of the project was to increase public awareness and to make the data more approachable, since the data typically is only meaningful to those in the astrophysics field in its raw form. The final product was able to ingest and display image and video analysis of solar weather patterns through publicly available endpoints from the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Association (NOAA) and the Space Weather Prediction Center (SWPC). The application targeted the Meta Quest family of devices and is available on the Meta Quest store for free.
